Definitions. A host is any speaker whose label starts with host (host_1, host_2, ...); a guest is any speaker whose label starts with guest (guest_1, guest_2, ...); the numbers only tell people apart. A non-host speaker is any speaker with a label that does not start with host, guests included. Each label is one speaker. Listeners are the audience of the podcast. 'Later' means in a later line. 'Explicitly' means in words that name the act. Previous episodes and talk that happened before the recording are outside this episode. Speaker labels are taken as written. Where an episode has several instances, the option that needs 'every' applies only if all instances meet it. 'After another subject has been discussed in between' means that between the two points the speakers said at least two full sentences about something else. The main conversation starts when a host first names what the episode is about, after greetings, introductions and any list of what the episode will cover.
